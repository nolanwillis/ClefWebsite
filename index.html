Dev Log

Notes and updates on the development of Clef.

The Team

Nolan Willis

Game Developer

Just me (for now). My journey into gaming started on a beat-up Nintendo 64, playing Knockout Kings 2000 with my brothers. I always wondered how games worked—how developers built expansive worlds and enemies that intelligently fought back—from the yarn-covered worlds of Kirby’s Epic Yarn to the raids of Destiny 2. After playing Overwatch on a club esports team while studying Computer Science at Syracuse University, I decided to focus my graduate studies on Game Programming. At DePaul University, I specialized in real-time systems and engine programming while building projects across Unreal Engine, Unity, and Godot.

After spending over a year running into walls with traditional software job hunts and shelving too many web development projects, I decided to step back from the applications and build my own game. Right now, the goal is to craft a solid vertical slice, with hopes of bringing more developers on board down the road.

Be sure to check out the weekly dev logs to follow along!
